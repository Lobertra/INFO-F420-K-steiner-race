Experiment

This page runs the experiment of the report directly in your browser: 5,400 races on random terminals (n = 4 to 12, k = 1 to 4, 300 races per row for k = 1 and 100 for k ≥ 2), with the same code as the race (race/steiner.js). For each row, it measures the share of X candidates pruned by each method and the work saved by the new method.

It takes less than a minute. The random terminals are always the same, so the results are always the same.
